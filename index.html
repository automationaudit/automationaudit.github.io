The 72-hour build

Designed to earn trust fast.

The first engagement is intentionally narrow. We do not ask you to overhaul your business. We identify the first high-friction workflow, implement the smallest useful fix, test it, and hand it off clearly.

Map the leak

We inspect where the repetitive work starts, where it should land, and what breaks when it depends on memory.

Pick the win

We choose the workflow with the best mix of frequency, pain, simplicity, and measurable usefulness.

Build the fix

We implement with free or existing tools first: forms, sheets, email rules, reminders, simple scripts, or your current CRM.

Hand it off

You get a short walkthrough, a disable path, and the next automation opportunities ranked by ROI.

No AI hype. Just operational leverage.
